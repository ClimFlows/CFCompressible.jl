CFCompressible.FC2D — Type
model = FC2D((; mgr, domain, space, fluid, spatial_scheme, vortex_scheme, thermoflux_scheme, viscosity_scheme,
                boundary_conditions))

Fully compressible binary fluid in a 2D box (x, z): prognostic m = (ρ, ρ consvar, ρ q) at cell centers and the velocity (u, w) on faces. spatial_scheme (e.g. Centred2()) sets the interpolations and differences of the C-grid, vortex_scheme the vortex force.

CFCompressible.loop — Method
state = loop(state, model::FC2D, time_scheme, params; outputs = nothing, book = diagnostics(model))

Main time integration loop for the FC2D model; advances state in place and returns it.

  • model: FC2D model instance
  • state: initial state (see initialize), overwritten by the final state; copy it first to keep it
  • time_scheme: time integration scheme
  • params: (; Nslice, slicesize, dtmax, cfl): Nslice time slices of slice_size [s], each split into equal steps of at most dt_max [s] and cfl (≤ 1 for stability) times the stability limit of time_scheme, see cfl_condition
  • outputs: callable outputs(model, state, dstate, scratch, book, t), called at t = 0 and at the end of each time slice, with the diagnostics book and the time t [s] (e.g. to record diagnostics), then closed with close(outputs) when the run ends, even on error; nothing records nothing
  • book: the diagnostics CookBook handed to outputs, e.g. one with more diagnostics than diagnostics(model); built once, as each session of it overwrites the same output arrays

CFCompressible.BoxDynamics.cons_flux_bc — Method
flux = cons_flux_bc(bc, value, thermo, i_cell, wall, model, qflux)

Flux of consvar on a wall face: the prescribed flux F of ρ consvar (Flux), the prescribed heat flux F (HeatFlux), or the face flux with the wall value cons_w at distance d and Twall = T(pwall, consw, qcell) (Value). The wall delivers exactly the heat flux (HeatFlux, or Value with the consistent scheme): the production between the wall and the cell center is inside consflux.

source
CFCompressible.BoxDynamics.dissipative_heating! — Method
ρε, σ_cons = dissipative_heating!((ρε, σ_cons), model::FC2D, thermoflux_scheme, state, (uτ_x, uτ_z, F_x, F_z), thermo,
                                  (diffusive_heating_x, diffusive_heating_z))

At the interior cells: the viscous heating ρε = ∇·(u·τ) - u·F [W m⁻³], and the production of consvar σ = (ρε + ℑ(diffusive heating)) / conjvar with ThermoFluxConsistent, or σ = 0 with ThermoFluxDowngradient. Zero-gradient halo. Wall faces carry no diffusive heating (zero-gradient halo of conjvar, chempot): the energy a wall delivers is conjvarcell consflux + chempotcell qflux, see cons_flux_bc.

CFCompressible.BoxDynamics.fluxes! — Method
consflux_x, consflux_z, qflux_x, qflux_z, diffusive_heating_x, diffusive_heating_z = fluxes!(outputs, model::FC2D, state, thermo)

Irreversible fluxes on the faces: qflux = -ρ kq (∂q - Γ ∂p) and consflux (carrying the heat flux -ρ Cp kT ∂T, or down the gradient of consvar, see CFDiffusionSchemes.consvar_flux), ρ and coefficients averaged from the two cells; on wall faces, from apply_tracer_bcs!. Also the diffusive heating -(consflux ∂conjvar + qflux ∂chempot), zero on wall faces (zero-gradient halo of conjvar, chempot). Periodized.

CFCompressible.BoxDynamics.kinematics! — Method
∂u_x, ∂w_z, ∂u_z, ∂w_x, K = kinematics!((∂u_x, ∂w_z, ∂u_z, ∂w_x, K), model::FC2D, state)

Velocity derivatives and kinetic energy per unit mass: at the cells ∂u/∂x = δx u / Δx, ∂w/∂z = δz w / Δz and K = (ℑx(u²) + ℑz(w²)) / 2; at the vertices ∂u/∂z = δZ u / Δz and ∂w/∂x = δX w / Δx. Periodized.

CFCompressible.BoxDynamics.state_bc! — Method
state = state_bc!(state, model::FC2D)

Fill the halo of state = (; m, u, w), never its interior: the ghost velocities realize the wall conditions (see fill_velocity_halo!), and m gets a zero-gradient halo, so that the equation of state only sees valid states. The tracer fluxes through walls are set by apply_tracer_bcs!, not through the halo.

CFCompressible.BoxDynamics.thermodynamics! — Method
p, consvar, q, T, conjvar, chempot, Cp, ∂consvar_∂q, Γ, B = thermodynamics!(outputs, model::FC2D, state)

Pointwise thermodynamics from m = (ρ, ρ consvar, ρ q), on the interior cells: p(v, consvar, q), consvar, q, T, conjvar, chempot, Cp, ∂consvar/∂q, Γ = -∂μ/∂p / ∂μ/∂q (barodiffusion) and B = h - conjvar consvar - chempot q, the potential of the momentum forcing. Zero-gradient halo: every difference across a wall vanishes.

CFCompressible.BoxDynamics.velocity_tendencies! — Method
du, dw = velocity_tendencies!((du, dw), model::FC2D, state, thermo, (K, vortex_x, vortex_z, F_x, F_z))

Velocity tendencies on the faces, -consvar ∇conjvar - q ∇chempot - ∇B - g ẑ - ∇K - ω×u + F/ρ, with the face values ρ = ℑ(m₁) and consvar, q = ℑ(m₂)/ρ, ℑ(m₃)/ρ.

CFCompressible.BoxDynamics.viscous_stress! — Method
uτ_x, uτ_z, F_x, F_z, wτ_xz, uτ_xz = viscous_stress!(outputs, model::FC2D, state, (∂u_x, ∂w_z, ∂u_z, ∂w_x))

Viscous stress for a constant dynamic viscosity μ and the Stokes hypothesis, τ = 2μ (S - (∇·u) I / 3): on the faces, the viscous force F = ∇·τ (per unit volume) and the energy flux u·τ; on the vertices, the shear-stress work ℑX(w) τxz and ℑZ(u) τxz. The dissipation ρε = ∇·(u·τ) - u·F is formed in dissipative_heating!.

CFCompressible.BoxInitialize.anchored_column — Method
ρ = anchored_column(model::FC2D, consvar, q, pref, jc)

balanced_column with the pressure pref at the height z0 (see balanced_density): the pressure ptop in the top cell is found by secant iterations on the pressure at z0, which depends on ptop almost one for one.

source
CFCompressible.BoxInitialize.balanced_column — Method
ρ = balanced_column(model::FC2D, consvar, q, ptop, jc)

Densities ρ[j] of a column at rest in discrete balance: the vertical momentum forcing of the model vanishes on every interior face, given the conservative variable and composition of the interior cells jc, and the pressure ptop [Pa] in the top cell. Marches down, solving for each density with bracketed_root: in floating point the forcing is a staircase in ρ (its terms, of size h ~ c_p T, cancel), on which a plain secant iteration may wander without converging.

source
CFCompressible.BoxInitialize.balanced_density — Method
ρ = balanced_density(model::FC2D; consvar, q, pref)

Density of the fluid at rest with the tracers consvar(x, z) and q(x, z), on all cells (zero-gradient halo): each column is in discrete hydrostatic balance (the model's vertical momentum forcing vanishes on every interior face), with pressure pref [Pa] at the height space.z0 where the geopotential vanishes. As for the anelastic reference state, the pressure at z0 is that of the piecewise-linear interpolant of the pressures of the cell centers (extrapolated linearly from the two nearest interior cells if z0 is outside the box).

source
CFCompressible.BoxInitialize.bracketed_root — Method
x = bracketed_root(f, x0, x1)

A root of f from the first guesses x0 and x1, to the resolution of the floating-point numbers: secant iterations until the root is bracketed (f changes sign between the last two iterates), then bisection of the bracket down to two adjacent numbers, returning the one where |f| is least. Without a bracket after 50 secant iterations (or once the secant stalls, f being the same at both iterates), the iterate of least |f| met so far. Unlike a plain secant, it cannot end on an arbitrary iterate when f is a staircase at round-off.

CFCompressible.BoxInitialize.divergence_free_velocity — Method
(u, w) = divergence_free_velocity(model::FC2D, ρ, ψ)

Velocity on the faces with discretely divergence-free mass flux, ∇·(ρu) = 0 in every cell, for the density ρ (e.g. from balanced_density), from a mass streamfunction ψ(X, Z) [kg m⁻¹ s⁻¹] evaluated at the cell vertices: ρu = δz ψ / Δz on u-faces and ρw = -δx ψ / Δx on w-faces (ρ averaged from the two cells). The velocity through a wall vanishes where ψ is constant along it. Faces without two neighboring cells (halo edges) are zero.

CFCompressible.Loops.max_buoyancy_frequency — Method
N = max_buoyancy_frequency(model::FC2D, m)

max(N) [s⁻¹] over the w-faces J between interior cells J-1 and J, N² = g (α ∂consvar/∂z - β ∂q/∂z), with α = ∂v/∂consvar / v and β = -∂v/∂q / v at the face (mean pressure, consvar and q of the two cells); N² < 0 (static instability) does not oscillate and gives 0.

source
CFCompressible.Loops.max_frequency — Method
ω = max_frequency(model::FC2D, state)

Return an upper bound ω [s⁻¹] on the fastest discrete frequency of the state over the interior, the sum of:

  • advection, centered on the C-grid: max|u|/dx + max|w|/dz;
  • sound, on the C-grid (ω² = c² (4/dx² sin²(k dx/2) + 4/dz² sin²(m dz/2))): 2 max(c) √(1/dx² + 1/dz²);
  • diffusion: 4 max(ν, kT, kq) (1/dx² + 1/dz²), with ν = (4μ/3) / min(ρ), the compressive modes being damped by (4μ/3) ∇(∇·u);
  • internal gravity waves (ω = N |kₓ|/|k| ≤ N): max(N), see max_buoyancy_frequency.

CFCompressible.diagnostics — Method
book = diagnostics(model::FC2D)

CookBook of the FC2D diagnostics, opened with open(book; state, dstate, scratch, model). Names, long names and units: see diagnostic_info (or describe_diagnostics(model)). Debugging intermediates: internal_diagnostics.

Build the book once and reuse it: it owns the output array of each computed diagnostic, allocated on its grid location on first use, which every session of the book overwrites. The other diagnostics are the arrays (or views) of the state, tendency and scratch. Copy a result to keep it beyond the next session.
